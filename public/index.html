Benchle

Two bench-press puzzles a day. Everyone gets the same bars, and new ones load at midnight.

Bar count

You see one side of a loaded bench bar. Enter the total weight, bar included.

Work in

Two lifters share one bar. Pick the plate to pull off each side so the next lifter's weight is ready.

The clock runs while you answer. Each mistake adds 5 seconds. Three mistakes on one bar and the answer is shown.
